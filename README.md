# Gorn Fishing 🎣

The official app of **CoPCO — the Co-operative of Pleasure Craft Owners**: Merv, Ace, Northy and Bouncing, four mates who went halves-of-halves on a second-hand cabin cruiser from Bouncing's Uncle Uncle.

**Live:** https://travis-coder712.github.io/gorn-fishing/ — open on your phone and *Add to Home Screen*.

## What's in it
- **The Boat** — photos, what we know, history, value
- **Get Legal** — marine & fishing licences, rego, insurance, safety gear (Victoria, costs + timings)
- **On the Water** — towing, ramps near Seaford, launching tips, maiden-voyage checklist
- **Get it Ready** — recommission log, mechanic checklist, won't-start troubleshooter
- **Seasons** — what to chase in Port Phillip Bay month by month, bag limits
- **Conditions** — the live **Gorn Index**: wind, gusts, waves, tide, pressure, moon & season scored 0–100 (Open-Meteo, refreshes on open)
- **Money** — expense tracker split four ways with settle-up
- **To-do** — jobs and shopping lists
- **Upgrades** — cover, cabin refit, fridge, fishing gear, a voting wishlist

## Merch
CoPCO logo files are in [`assets/`](assets/): full-colour SVG, 3000px PNG for printing, and a 2-colour (navy + white) version for screen printing.
